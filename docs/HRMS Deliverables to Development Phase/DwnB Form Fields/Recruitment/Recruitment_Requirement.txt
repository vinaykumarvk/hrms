Below is the detailed end-to-end recruitment configuration and implementation walkthrough.

🎯 Recruitment Overview
Recruitment in Darwinbox covers the full hiring lifecycle — from raising a requisition to onboarding handover.
📍 Admin Path: Darwinbox > Admin Access > Settings > Talent > Talent Acquisition
📍 User Path: My Access > Recruitment

2️⃣ Requisition (Position Demand)
📍 Path: Dashboard > Recruitment > Requisitions > + Add Requisition
Steps:

Permission role holder clicks + Add Requisition.
Fill Step 1: Requisition Details (department, designation, location, etc.).
Step 2: Job Details – Specify number of positions, assign Hiring Lead, provide justification.
Submit for approval to designated stakeholders (configured via approval flow).
Once approved, the requisition can be linked to a Job.


3️⃣ Job Creation & Recruiter Assignment
After requisition approval:

Go to Dashboard > Recruitment > Requisitions.
For the approved requisition, click the kebab icon (⋮) → Activate → Assign Recruiter.
The Hiring Lead/Admin associates positions with a Job Opening.

Job Creation Steps (typically 5 steps):

Step 1: Job Details – Title, description, department, location, employment type.
Step 2: Hiring Workflow – Define stages such as Application → Screening → Interview → Offer → Hired.
Step 3: Job Posting – Configure where to post (Careers Page, Internal Job Posting, Referrals).
Step 4: Application Form – Define the application form candidates fill.
Step 5: Review & Publish – Final review before publishing.


4️⃣ Job Posting Channels
Once the job is created, it can be posted on multiple channels:

Careers Page (External) – Public-facing job board for external candidates.
Internal Job Posting (IJP) – Visible only to employees who meet eligibility criteria.
Referrals (Refer) – Employees refer candidates and earn rewards.
External Recruiter Portal – Vendors/agencies can submit candidates.
Job Boards / Social Media – Via integrations.


5️⃣ Candidate Addition
Candidates can be added through:

Apply via Careers Page – External candidate self-apply.
Manual Addition by Recruiter – Recruitment > Jobs > Select Job > Add Candidate.
Bulk Import – Upload multiple candidates via import template.
Resume Parsing (Document Parsing) – Auto-fill candidate profile from resume.
Employee Referral – Through the Refer module.
External Recruiter Portal – Vendors push candidates.

Duplicity Check runs automatically based on configured fields (email, phone, etc.) to prevent duplicate profiles.

6️⃣ Candidate Lifecycle / Hiring Workflow
Candidates move through the configured Hiring Workflow stages:

Applied → Initial application received.
Screening – Resume review.
Pre-Screening – Optional automated screening based on configured criteria.
Assessment – External/internal assessment.
Interview Rounds – Schedule and conduct multiple rounds.
Offer Proposal – Internal approval of compensation.
Offer Letter – Generate and release offer letter.
Pre-Offer Stage → Pending List (Yet to Onboard) – Transition to onboarding.

Candidate Status Management:

Status Copy/Move – Move candidates to other jobs.
Reject – With configurable reasons.
Hold – Pause processing.


7️⃣ Interview Scheduling
📍 Path: Candidate Profile > Schedule Interview
Steps:

Select Interview Type (Technical, HR, Managerial).
Choose Interview Mode (In-person, Telephonic, Video).
Add Interviewers – calendar invite is sent automatically.
Set Date, Time, Location/Link.
Attach Interview Guide / Evaluation Form.
Send invite — candidate and interviewer receive notifications.

Interviewers can submit Evaluation Feedback post-interview through their dashboard.

8️⃣ Offer Proposal & Offer Letter
📍 Path: Candidate Profile > Initiate Offer
Offer Letter Configuration (3 Steps):

Job/Candidate Details – Verify candidate and job details.
Offer Letter Template – Select the configured HTML/DOCX template; placeholders auto-fill from candidate data.
Pay Details – Salary structure, components, and breakdown.

Approval Flow:

Configured approvers (e.g., Hiring Manager, HR, Finance) approve sequentially or in parallel.
Once approved → Offer Letter is generated and released to the candidate.
Candidate can Accept/Reject the offer via the Candidate Portal (with optional Digital Signature).


9️⃣ Pre-Offer / Yet to Onboard / Handover to Onboarding
Once the candidate accepts the offer:

Status changes to Pre-Offer / Yet to Onboard.
Candidate appears under Admin Access > Onboarding > Yet to Onboard tab.
The Onboarding process can be Initiated from here (linking recruitment data to onboarding forms).


🔟 Permissions & Roles
Recruitment uses both Standard and Custom Permissions:

Recruitment Admin – Full control.
Hiring Lead – Manage their requisitions/jobs.
Recruiter – Manage assigned jobs and candidates.
Interviewer – View assigned candidates, submit feedback.
External Recruiter – Limited access via external portal.

Permissions can be scoped by Assignment Framework (Department, Location, Company, etc.).

1️⃣1️⃣ Notifications & Email Templates
Configured notifications include:

Requisition Approval/Rejection
Job Posted (IJP, Careers)
Candidate Application Acknowledgment
Interview Schedule / Reschedule / Cancellation
Offer Letter Release / Acceptance / Rejection
Recruiter / Hiring Manager Task Alerts

Templates can be standard or custom, with dynamic placeholders.

1️⃣2️⃣ Recruitment Reports & Audit Logs

Reports – Requisition reports, candidate funnel, source effectiveness, time-to-hire, recruiter performance.
Audit Logs – Track all changes to requisitions, jobs, candidate status, offer letters.
