# R4 — Identifier-Registry Consistency & Collisions (Integration Review)

**Scope:** 14 platform-grounded government-HRMS BRDs `docs/brd/v3/G01..G14*.md` + `docs/brd/PLATFORM_FOUNDATION.md`.
**Concern audited (single cross-cutting):** namespaced-id correctness, cross-module id collisions, role / capability-flag conflicts, and standard-error-code conformance.
**Date:** 2026-07-01 · **Reviewer role:** integration reviewer (report-only, no repair applied).

## Verdict

The id registry is **largely consistent and the platform re-grounding is genuine** — the namespaced `ERR-G##-*`, `VAL-G##-*`, `JOB-G##-*`, `MSG-G##-*`, `WF-G##-*` scheme is followed by most modules, shared platform ids (`VAL-PAN/AADHAAR/EFFECTIVE`, `JOB-M01/M04/M05/M09/M11-*`, `MSG-SYS-JOBFAIL`, `ERR-FORBIDDEN/LOADFAIL/PRECOND/DUP-INSTANCE`) are **cited rather than redefined**, and every legacy non-platform code (`VALIDATION_ERROR(400)`, `AUTH_REQUIRED(401)`, `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE(503)`, body `requestId`) is correctly confined to the documented v2→v3 reconciliation/override tables in G02/G04/G05/G06/G07/G08/G09 — **with one leak**. The material problems are concentrated in **two modules (G09, G13) that never adopted the `ERR-G##-*` namespace** and instead define dozens of bare `UPPER_SNAKE` module error codes in their live API/LLD sections, which simultaneously breaks the namespacing convention, leaks an invented code, and creates a cross-module `SIGNATURE_INVALID` collision. Secondary findings are an unowned/dangling `JOB-G01-EFFDATE` cited 24× by G02 but never registered by its owner G01, fragmentation of the single SR-ledger append validation into three ids, and divergent role codes for the one platform "Appointing Authority" actor. None are data-destructive; all are registry-hygiene defects that should be fixed before contracts freeze.

**Counts by severity:** High 2 · Medium 5 · Low 2 (total 9).

### Positive conformance notes (no finding)
- **Legacy-code amendment discipline is good.** All `VALIDATION_ERROR(400)`, `AUTH_REQUIRED(401)`, `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE(503)`, `requestId`-body occurrences in G02/G04/G05/G06/G07/G08/G09 sit inside explicit "v2→v3 override / reconciliation §C / amendment" rows — except the single G09:2251 live leak captured as Finding 1.
- **Shared-id reuse is correct** where present: `VAL-PAN`(16), `VAL-AADHAAR`(11), `VAL-EFFECTIVE`(95), `JOB-M01-EFFDATE`(7) and other `JOB-M0x-*`, `MSG-SYS-JOBFAIL`(60), and the Foundation `ERR-FORBIDDEN/LOADFAIL/PRECOND/DUP-INSTANCE` family are cited, never re-authored.
- **No own-G-code mislabelling** found in the live spec bodies of G01,G03,G04,G05,G07,G08,G10,G11,G14 — each uses its own G-code for its `ERR/VAL/JOB/MSG` ids; cross-G references in G02 (`JOB-G01-*`, `VAL-G12-*`) and G06 (`VAL-G12-*`) are deliberate consumer citations, addressed under Findings 4–5.

Platform shared-id list taken from `PLATFORM_FOUNDATION.md` §4.2 (8-code table), §4.3 (shared `ERR-*`), §7 (`VAL-*`), §8.3 (id conventions), §6 (RBAC additions).
